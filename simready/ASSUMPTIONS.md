# Evidence and assumptions

This derivative is an independent, provisional reconstruction. “Published” below refers to manufacturer information; it is not a measurement of the modeled unit. “Study” identifies a parameter selected for the model. No physical unit, engineering drawing with end datums, or manufacturer mechanism data was available for this continuation.

| Topic | Evidence or assumption | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall size | The [seca product sheet](https://hs.seca.com/hubfs/Lyndsey%20Folders/Product%20Sheets/seca_Scale-up_Line_654_455_257_257sh_product_sheet_US_en_24.pdf?hsLang=en) lists 801 × 650 × 2387 mm for the configured device. The selected reconstruction measures approximately 801 × 582.7 × 2387 mm. | The 67.3 mm depth discrepancy is unresolved. The model was not stretched; front-aligned, centered and rear-aligned intervals in `research/` are comparison guides, not proposed physical corrections. |
| Platform | The product sheet lists 600 × 467 × 70 mm. The modeled lower chassis is approximately 600 × 467 × 54 mm and the modeled glass top is approximately 73 mm above the floor. | The reference for the published 70 mm dimension is unknown; neither figure alone establishes a platform error. |
| Wheels | The manufacturer describes two casters for tilting and moving the equipment. The 43 mm modeled wheel radius, X-axis pivots and rotating geometry come from the existing reconstruction. | Wheel size, axle positions, exact mechanism, load and traction require independent measurement. The visual tread rotates; other hub/guard pieces follow the source's existing grouping. |
| Display | The two pivot locations and axes were inherited from the study controls; the limits are hypothetical ±0.6 rad swivel and ±0.4 rad tilt. | Actual display degrees of freedom, stops, hinge resistance, cable motion and clearances are unverified. The cable is static. |
| Mass and inertia | The product sheet lists 29 kg; the five-body distribution is a study estimate: 27.8 kg base, 0.1 kg per wheel, 0.1 kg swivel collar and 0.9 kg display. Centers of mass and principal moments are calculated from simplified estimated shapes. | Valid numerical inertia and a correct total do not establish the actual mass distribution, balance, forces or stability. |
| Contact materials | Static/dynamic friction coefficients are estimated respectively as rubber 0.70/0.55, coating 0.50/0.40, glass 0.40/0.30 and plastic 0.45/0.35, all with restitution 0.02. | No material, friction, restitution or floor interaction has been measured; the local Bullet reconstruction instead uses a single friction setting. |
| Visual materials | Glass, white finishes, metal, polymers and screen appearance are translated preview approximations; semantic `unknown` is retained where composition is not established. | Their spectral, optical and target-renderer behavior are unverified; they are not a material bill of materials. |
| Collision exclusions | Joint-connected links do not collide, and the display's simplified collider is additionally filtered against the simplified mounting-arm collider. | Mast and rail contacts remain eligible in the USD. This targeted filter is a proxy modeling choice, not evidence of a real joint clearance. |
| Unpowered display | The main USD has no display drives. In a reconstructed Bullet gravity/drop test the display fell to the approximate tilt stop. | Position retention under gravity is not solved in the main stage. The result is a local proxy test, not a direct PhysX test. |
| Illustrative display drive | `usd/layers/illustrative_driven.usda` adds estimated zero-target spring/servo-like USD angular drives: swivel stiffness 0.05 N·m/degree, damping 0.005 N·m·s/degree, max torque 1.0 N·m; tilt 0.10, 0.010 and 1.5 respectively. | The overlay tends to center the display and does not model validated passive hinge friction. It is not validated in PhysX or shown to hold the display. A separate ideal one-axis calculation approaches a 0.47 degree equilibrium using the assumed mass and center of mass, while different Bullet spring approximations failed the hold criterion; see `validation/revisions/spring-first/` and the other spring reports. |
| Render geometry | The USD render inventory contains 272 meshes and 145,872 triangles; 22 meshes have indexed boundary edges and 220 triangles have exactly zero computed area. Another near-zero threshold marks 3,004 triangles total. | Open text and art can be intentional. No automatic repair was applied because zero-area faces can connect coincident-index seams; inspect and weld deliberately before removing them. Colliders are independently checked closed. |
| Synthetic workflow | The separate portable study accepts only invented inputs and uses local deterministic mock transfer timing. | It is an independent subset, not parity with the Blender simulator, a USD screen adapter, real firmware, patient-data workflow, sensor model or a network service. |
