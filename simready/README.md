# seca Scale-up Ultra articulated study

This package extends the earlier parked SimReady-style derivative with four provisional revolute joints, while preserving the original Blender file. The main USD passes the **Robotics-Prop 4.0.0 static validation profile**; that is a schema/content result, not a runtime, mechanical, manufacturer or medical validation.

## Open the files

- Main, unpowered OpenUSD: `asset/seca_scale_up_ultra_articulated/usd/seca_scale_up_ultra_articulated.usda`. Keep the adjacent `layers`, `licenses`, `.thumbs` and notice files with it. Units are meters, Z is up, and the saved pose is floor centered.
- Editable Blender study: `source/seca-articulation-editable.blend`. In Object mode, select the existing `CONTROL • wheel L/R` objects and rotate local X, or select `CONTROL • display swivel` (local Z) and `CONTROL • display tilt and rotation` (local X). The display controls have provisional limits. The file contains the original embedded study scripts; inspect and explicitly run only scripts you trust. Loading and the checks here disable script autorun.
- Preserved original: `source/seca-scale-up-ultra-original.blend`, SHA-256 `57e64f249073c472abacbc942eb990a76657a26333f56e80ec922815e7c5525d`. The original checkout and the previous derivative were not modified. The previous derivative's build inputs needed for the continuation are under `source/build-inputs/`.
- Optional, unvalidated centering-drive overlay: `asset/seca_scale_up_ultra_articulated/usd/layers/illustrative_driven.usda`. Open this stage explicitly to experiment with its two artificial USD spring/servo-like angular drives. It is a separate layer, not a measured model of the real display or a confirmed fix for position holding.
- Fictional offline behavior subset: `source/synthetic_study/study.py`. It accepts synthetic weight, tare, hold, units and `SYNTH-<digits>` scan fixtures and exercises a deterministic local transfer mock. `python3 scripts/run_synthetic_study.py` runs its scenarios. It does not connect to the USD display or Blender scene and does not reproduce all of the original simulator.

## What is known and what remains open

The selected geometry is approximately 801 × 582.7 × 2387 mm; the [manufacturer's product sheet](https://hs.seca.com/hubfs/Lyndsey%20Folders/Product%20Sheets/seca_Scale-up_Line_654_455_257_257sh_product_sheet_US_en_24.pdf?hsLang=en) gives 801 × 650 × 2387 mm. The missing 67.3 mm cannot be registered to physical front/rear surfaces from the available evidence. `research/depth-registration.png`, `.json` and guide USD show three hypothetical registrations; none is a geometry change or a physical clearance.

There are five estimated rigid links and 42 convex collision proxies. The two modeled wheels rotate about X. The display study uses swivel about Z and tilt about X with limits ±0.6 rad and ±0.4 rad. Total mass is the published 29 kg, while the body distribution, inertia, contact parameters, display joint geometry and limits are estimates. `ASSUMPTIONS.md` gives details and the distinction between published and modeled quantities.

The base USD's display joints are unpowered. A local five-body Bullet gravity/drop reconstruction settled on the floor but the display fell to its modeled tilt stop. An attempted Bullet spring approximation also failed to hold it near neutral; this does not establish how the optional USD drives behave. The unpowered and failed spring results are retained in `validation/` and `validation/revisions/`. The display cable is static, and the optional drive overlay centers the display rather than reproducing known passive friction.

## Evidence

[`validation/public-checks.json`](validation/public-checks.json) summarizes checks run on an isolated copy of the repository candidate.

- [`validation/simready-final.json`](validation/simready-final.json) and its HTML report contain the NVIDIA SimReady validator 2026.8.0 Robotics-Prop 4.0.0 static result. The validator also reports the optional FET_004_STANDARD and FET_004_PHYSX static feature checks as passed; FET_100_ISAAC and other listed optional features remain unmet. No Isaac Sim, RTX, PhysX dynamics or NVIDIA benchmark was run.
- `validation/articulation-checks.json` verifies the 272 selected visual components' bounds and material bindings, five bodies, four joint frames/axes/study limits, 29 kg sum, positive consistent inertia, closed proxy topology, selective filter, drive-layer placement and resolved dependencies. It does not prove exact mesh equality or real-world fidelity.
- `validation/control-sweep.json` records 49 Blender source-control display poses and four wheel angles against selected surfaces; `validation/proxy-sweep.json` records 49 sampled USD-proxy poses. These do not prove full containment, continuous swept clearance, cable routing or physical clearance.
- `validation/bullet-joints.json` records both directions for each reconstructed Bullet hinge: more than one wheel revolution and at least 90% of each provisional display limit. The base is fixed and gravity is off. Because Blender's compound-collider representation cannot implement the USD per-shape pair filter, the mounting-arm collider was omitted entirely in these Bullet tests; Bullet also calculates its own inertia. These are not executions of the USD physics or PhysX.
- `validation/analytic-display-drive.json` evaluates the hypothetical tilt drive in a separate ideal fixed-base, one-axis gravity model: all three starting angles approach about 0.47 degrees under its assumed constants. This excludes impacts, link motion, contacts, USD, PhysX and physical evidence; it does not supersede the failed Bullet hold experiments.
- `validation/revisions/unpowered/bullet-settle.json` is the unpowered five-body 10 cm drop; `validation/bullet-settle-with-hold.json`, `validation/bullet-settle-spring2.json` and `validation/bullet-settle-combined-spring2.json` record failed qualitative spring-hold experiments. Their settings differ from the USD overlay and provide no validation of it.
- `validation/rebuild-check.json` records a clean rebuild from the included inputs. Its seven authored USD layer texts match the delivered layers, while the five binary crate encodings differ; an earlier byte-identical build is retained separately and byte-for-byte reproducibility is not guaranteed. `validation/visual-topology.json` is an indexed render-mesh inventory, not a solid/clearance test. `validation/blender-reopen.json` checks controls and preservation of prior text blocks with scripts disabled. `validation/synthetic-study.json` records limited offline state scenarios. `previews/` contains Blender CPU renders after USD import, a collision-proxy view and a graph derived from the joint data. The posed previews manually transform imported visuals and are not physics simulations.
- `BACKLOG-STATUS.md`, `.csv` and `.json` retain all 28 items with their current states and remaining work. Selected failed intermediate attempts are kept under `validation/revisions/`; the full local delivery retains the remaining development history.

## Rebuilding and packaging

Scripts resolve paths relative to this folder. Rebuilding needs Blender 5.2.2 and an engineering Python with OpenUSD, NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib; static profile validation additionally needs the specified NVIDIA validator and tier. The saved scripts write their named outputs; work in a new copy and preserve existing evidence first. The validator environment itself is not included. The repository contains the asset, editable source, build inputs, selected failure evidence and the validation records; a successful rebuild or hash match does not prove source truth or runtime acceptance.
