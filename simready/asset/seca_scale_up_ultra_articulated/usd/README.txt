seca Scale-up Ultra articulated study, 2026-10-30

Open seca_scale_up_ultra_articulated.usda with adjacent layers and .thumbs.
The USD uses meters, Z-up, five estimated rigid bodies and four revolute joints.
It passes Robotics-Prop 4.0.0 static validation, not a dynamics/runtime test.
The main stage has unpowered display joints and will not hold a pose under all
gravity conditions. Display swivel +/-0.6 rad, tilt +/-0.4 rad, wheel and display
pivots, body mass distribution, inertias, contacts and materials are estimates.

layers/illustrative_driven.usda is an optional, unvalidated centering-drive
study. It is not evidence of manufacturer motors, springs, friction or limits.
Published overall depth is 650 mm while this model is 582.7 mm; the physical
front/rear dimensional datums remain unresolved. Do not interpret the study as
clinical, engineering, manufacturing or manufacturer validation. See the
package README and ASSUMPTIONS.md for evidence, tests and limitations.
